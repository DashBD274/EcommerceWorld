Platform Rules

Platform Rules & User Guidelines

Platform Rules & Working Guidelines

01. Account Registration

Please provide accurate and valid information when creating your account. Keep your login details confidential and secure.

02. Order Selection

Users can access available orders through their working account. Review the order details carefully before selecting an order.

03. Order Completion

Complete each selected order according to the instructions provided by the platform and submit the required information correctly.

04. Commission

Eligible completed orders may generate commission based on the applicable commission rate and current platform terms.

05. Working Balance

Users should maintain the required account balance for activities that require funds to process an order. Any balance movement should be recorded in the account transaction history.

06. Order Submission

Before submitting an order, verify all relevant details. Once submitted, the order may not be reversible depending on its status.

07. Withdrawal

Users may request withdrawal of eligible funds according to the platform's current withdrawal requirements, processing procedures, and supported payment methods.

08. Affiliate Program

Users participating in the Affiliate Program may receive referral rewards for eligible referrals in accordance with the current affiliate terms.

09. Account Security

Never share your password, verification code, wallet credentials, or other confidential account information with another person.

10. Fair Use

Fraud, unauthorized transactions, false information, order manipulation, or attempts to misuse the platform are strictly prohibited.

11. Maintenance & Updates

The platform may perform scheduled or emergency maintenance and system upgrades. Some services may be temporarily unavailable during this period.

12. Customer Support

For questions regarding orders, account balance, commissions, withdrawals, or affiliate activities, please contact Customer Support through the official support channel.

Payment Methods

Digital Currency Support

Crypto Payment & Deposit

Supported Payment Methods

Our platform supports deposits using selected digital currencies and blockchain networks. Available options may include USDT, USDC, TRC20, ERC20, and BEP20, depending on the payment method currently available on your account.

How to Deposit

Step 1 — Open Deposit

Log in to your account and navigate to Wallet → Deposit.

Step 2 — Select Cryptocurrency

Choose your preferred digital currency and select the correct blockchain network.

Step 3 — Copy the Deposit Address

Copy the wallet address displayed on the deposit page. Always verify the address and network before sending funds.

Step 4 — Send the Payment

Open your crypto wallet or exchange, enter the deposit address, select the same network, and enter the amount you want to deposit.

Step 5 — Confirm the Transaction

Review the currency, network, wallet address, and amount carefully before confirming the transfer.

Step 6 — Wait for Confirmation

After the blockchain transaction receives the required confirmations, the deposited amount will be credited to your platform account according to the platform's processing procedure.

Important

Always use the exact cryptocurrency and network shown on the deposit page. Sending funds through an unsupported network or to an incorrect address may result in permanent loss of funds.

Deposit Status

You can check your transaction status from Wallet → Deposit History. If a transaction remains pending beyond the normal processing time, contact Customer Support with the transaction hash (TXID).
