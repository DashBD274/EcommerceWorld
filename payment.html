Crypto Payment & Deposit

Supported Payment Methods

Our platform supports deposits using selected digital currencies and blockchain networks. Available options may include USDT, USDC, TRC20, ERC20, and BEP20, depending on the payment method currently available on your account.

How to Deposit

Step 1 — Open Deposit

Log in to your account and navigate to Wallet → Deposit.

Step 2 — Select Cryptocurrency

Choose your preferred digital currency and select the correct blockchain network.

Step 3 — Copy the Deposit Address

Copy the wallet address displayed on the deposit page. Always verify the address and network before sending funds.

Step 4 — Send the Payment

Open your crypto wallet or exchange, enter the deposit address, select the same network, and enter the amount you want to deposit.

Step 5 — Confirm the Transaction

Review the currency, network, wallet address, and amount carefully before confirming the transfer.

Step 6 — Wait for Confirmation

After the blockchain transaction receives the required confirmations, the deposited amount will be credited to your platform account according to the platform's processing procedure.

Important

Always use the exact cryptocurrency and network shown on the deposit page. Sending funds through an unsupported network or to an incorrect address may result in permanent loss of funds.

Deposit Status

You can check your transaction status from Wallet → Deposit History. If a transaction remains pending beyond the normal processing time, contact Customer Support with the transaction hash (TXID).
